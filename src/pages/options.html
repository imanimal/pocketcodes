AuthSkip

Sign-in codes from every inbox you add, in one list. It only reads mail, and nothing leaves your browser.

Installed
  1. Add your accounts below. Gmail needs a free Google sign-in client of your own first (the Gmail section walks you through it); iCloud needs the small helper program and an app-specific password.
  2. When a site sends you a code, it shows up in the toolbar button within a few seconds. Fill puts it into the page, or press Alt+Shift+F.
  3. Pick a look under Look: Standard, or one of the Pocket themes.
